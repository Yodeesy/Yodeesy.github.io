Publications & Working Papers

When to Intervene? State-Aware Sparse Manipulation in Federated Reinforcement Learning

ICLR 2027 Under Review

Shutong Zheng (First Author), et al.

Formalized intervention timing as a novel vulnerability dimension in FRL; developed V-BSA using policy-entropy gating and envelope-constrained behavioral steering to theoretically and empirically expose limits of robust aggregation rules.

Rethinking One-Shot Federated Graph Learning: Training-Free Statistical Estimation

AAAI 2027 Under Review

Shutong Zheng (First Author), et al.

Identified representation misalignment from local GNN training under extreme non-IID graphs; proposed SPEAR, a training-free framework computing topology-smoothed prototypes with adaptive shrinkage aggregation.

Structural Entropy-Driven Graph Diffusion Generation for One-Shot Federated Graph Learning

WSDM 2027 Under Review

Shutong Zheng* (Co-first Author), et al.

Resolved the volume-structure mismatch in one-shot FGL via structural entropy-based client differentiation; developed SPIRE, synthesizing topologically regularized pseudographs via server-side graph diffusion.
